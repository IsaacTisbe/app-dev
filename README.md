## My Favorite Series: **The Rookie**
**The Rookie** is a police drama that follows John Nolan, a 40-something man who becomes a the oldest rookie on the Los Angeles Police Department.

### Why I Like It
- Mix of action, humor, and heartfelt character moments
- Strong Chemistry among the cast
- Stories that balance everyday police work with personal growth
  
### Details
- Directed by Alexi Hawley
- Network **ABC**
- Genre is Police drama and comedy
- Premiere 2018
  
> A new chapter can start at any age.
